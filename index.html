The Story

Built from real-world experience.

JMR Apps didn't start in a boardroom. It started with 20+ years of hands-on experience in crane operations and lifting engineering — solving problems on job sites where the right tool simply didn't exist yet.

That same instinct — find the real problem, build the simplest thing that solves it — carried over when the work turned to software. Every product in this suite exists because someone needed it first.
