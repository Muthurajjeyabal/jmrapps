Overview

Everything a physical wall calendar shows — digital, and swipeable.

Nakshatra, rasi, thithi, yogam, karanam, rahu kalam, emakandam, nalla neram — the full daily panchangam, laid out the way a traditional Sun Prints calendar would, deity photo and all.

📅

Full daily panchangam

Nakshatra, rasi, thithi, yogam, karanam, ayanam, ritu, and nakshatra pada — every field a physical calendar shows.

🔮

12-rasi palan

Daily predictions for all twelve rasis, plus soolam direction and parigaram, and chandrashtamam nakshatra.

🗺️

All Tamil Nadu districts

Choose your district for accurate sunrise, sunset, rahu kalam, and nalla neram timings wherever you are.

👆

Swipe to change dates

Swipe right for the next day with a page-tearing animation, swipe left to go back — just like flipping a real calendar.

⏰

Life reminders

Birthday, anniversary, bill, EMI, medicine, and water reminders — built into the same app you check every morning.

🙏

Your own deity photo

Choose from many deity images for the calendar's hero — or upload your own photo from your gallery, just like a physical wall calendar.

📤

Share as an image

Share just the day's calendar — without the deity photo — as a clean image to family or group chats.

AI Astrologer

ஜோதிட சிகாமணி — a personal astrologer, always one tap away.

A floating chat icon on every screen opens a real natal-chart reading — not just today's panchangam.

Enter your birth date, time, and city — get your moon rasi, nakshatra, and lagnam computed for real, with current Guru and Sani transit analysis.

Ask life-decision questions — starting a business, taking a job, going abroad — answered against your own chart.

Find real upcoming muhurtham dates for marriage and griha pravesam, scanned from actual panchangam data, not guessed.

Get vastu door-direction guidance by rasi, and the right time for Paal Kaachal (housewarming) on any chosen date.
